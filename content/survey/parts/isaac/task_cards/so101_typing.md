P0三个状态为SEEK_PRESS→RELEASE_KEY→CLEARANCE。事件来自键盘真实关节行程：按下50%、释放25%构成滞回，两次连续logical采样；已按字符在松开时保留；clearance中任何新按键事件均错误。所有键都释放且爪尖高于目标4mm连续2控制tick才完成；**没有回初态、速度停稳或本项目100ms按压保持的同等要求**。代码明确Newton路径在manager边界按25Hz采样，不能声称逐物理子步检查，更不能把两次采样等同于经过独立500Hz oracle验证的80ms连续保持。[源码](https://github.com/NVIDIA/so101-typing-task/blob/7aec2ed26dce8c88ae007a2714680cce572e1466/source/so101_typing/tasks/mdp/commands/fixed_cartesian_typing_commands.py#L191-L343) [源码](https://github.com/NVIDIA/so101-typing-task/blob/7aec2ed26dce8c88ae007a2714680cce572e1466/source/so101_typing/tasks/fixed_cartesian_env_cfg.py#L35-L234) [源码](https://github.com/NVIDIA/so101-typing-task/blob/7aec2ed26dce8c88ae007a2714680cce572e1466/source/so101_typing/tasks/mdp/terminations.py#L54-L90)

定义`P_seek=I_same*I_phase0*clip((d_old−d)/.02,−1,1)`，`P_lift=I_same*I_phase≠0*clip((h−h_old)/.004,−1,1)`。`I_same`同时排除reset和阶段切换，防止换目标凭空创造进度。原配置Manager权重6.25、12.5乘dt=.04后：

`r=.25P_seek+.5P_lift+2E_down+E_up+E_clear+5I_complete−.0008 mean(Δa²)−.0004 mean((qdot/vmax)²)−.02S_scrape−5N_failure*(1−I_timeout)+0`。

其中`S_scrape=I_must_release*clip((.004−z_tip_key)/.004,0,1)*clip(v_lateral/.04,0,1)`。`N_failure`为配置列出的终止项数量（多项可叠加）。**上游timeout罚的注释与实际实现不符**：配置写−50（意图净−2），但固定运行时`is_terminated_term`把所有time_outs屏蔽，专门的timeout项恒0，且同tick的其他failure项也被屏蔽。已追到Docker固定祖先和MDP导入，patch未改该函数；这是源码推导，未执行上游轨迹复现。[源码](https://github.com/NVIDIA/so101-typing-task/blob/7aec2ed26dce8c88ae007a2714680cce572e1466/docker/typing-public.Dockerfile#L4-L18) [源码](https://github.com/NVIDIA/so101-typing-task/blob/7aec2ed26dce8c88ae007a2714680cce572e1466/source/so101_typing/tasks/mdp/__init__.pyi#L103-L105) [源码](https://github.com/ooctipus/IsaacLab/blob/6f991d4becf764b151e0a1c775561ddd9c406f72/source/isaaclab/isaaclab/envs/mdp/rewards.py#L43-L70) [源码](https://github.com/ooctipus/IsaacLab/blob/6f991d4becf764b151e0a1c775561ddd9c406f72/source/isaaclab/isaaclab/managers/reward_manager.py#L129-L159)**signed progress不是绝对接近分数，也不是带gamma修正并有策略不变性保证的PBRS。** 按下、松开、净空事件一次性发放。[源码](https://github.com/NVIDIA/so101-typing-task/blob/7aec2ed26dce8c88ae007a2714680cce572e1466/source/so101_typing/tasks/mdp/rewards.py#L137-L271) [源码](https://github.com/NVIDIA/so101-typing-task/blob/7aec2ed26dce8c88ae007a2714680cce572e1466/source/so101_typing/tasks/fixed_cartesian_env_cfg.py#L35-L234)
